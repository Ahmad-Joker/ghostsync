Support material for the iPhone test. A fixture here is not a camera result. Works offline: no network is used.

Keys: ← → or P N previous or next code in the class; S L switch class; + - change scale; A automatic scale. Link to one fixture: fixture-display.html#L03.

Keep the browser at 100% zoom. Scale is whole screen pixels per module with a 4-module white quiet zone. A scale below 5 makes the attempt INVALID: use a larger window and press A.
